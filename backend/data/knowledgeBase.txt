WEST BENGAL PUBLIC GRIEVANCE KNOWLEDGE BASE 
================================================== 
1. INTRODUCTION 
================================================== 
A public grievance is a complaint raised by a citizen regarding deficiencies in public services, infrastructure, 
utilities, sanitation, or government administration. 
The purpose of a grievance redressal system is to ensure that citizen complaints are recorded, processed, 
and resolved by the responsible authority. 
A grievance should include: 
• Description of the issue 
• Exact location 
• Date of occurrence 
• Supporting evidence such as photographs 
• Contact details of the complainant 
================================================== 
 
 
2. MUNICIPAL SERVICES GRIEVANCES 
Municipal grievances relate to services provided by local municipal corporations and municipalities. 
Common municipal grievances: 
• Garbage not collected 
• Overflowing garbage bins 
• Irregular street cleaning 
• Public toilet maintenance issues 
• Stray animal nuisance 
• Waterlogging 
• Drainage blockages 
• Illegal waste dumping 
Information required: 
• Ward number if known 
• Exact location 
• Nearby landmark 
• Description of issue 
• Supporting photographs 
Responsible Authority: Municipal Corporation or Municipality or Panchayat office 
==================================================

11. COMPLAINT FILING GUIDELINES 
A good complaint should: 
1. Clearly describe the issue. 
2. Mention exact location. 
3. Include date and time.

4. Attach photographs if available. 
5. Avoid abusive language.

12. ESCALATION PROCESS 
Step 1: 
Report issue to responsible department. 
Step 2: 
Obtain complaint reference number. 
Step 3: 
Track complaint status. 
Step 4: 
If unresolved, escalate to higher authority. 
Step 5: 
Submit supporting evidence during escalation. 
================================================== 
 
 
13. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
Q: What is a public grievance? 
A: A public grievance is a complaint regarding deficiencies in public services or infrastructure. 
Q: How do I report a pothole? 
A: Provide road name, location, nearby landmark, and photographs. 
Q: Who handles garbage complaints? 
A: Municipal authorities or Solid Waste Management Departments. 
Q: Who handles water supply issues? 
A: Water Supply Department or Municipality. 
Q: Why should I attach photographs? 
A: Photographs help authorities verify and assess issues faster. 
Q: What should I do if my complaint is not resolved? 
A: Escalate the complaint to the next level authority with supporting evidence. 
================================================== 
 
 
14. CITIZEN RESPONSIBILITIES 
Citizens should: 
• Provide accurate information. 
• Avoid duplicate complaints. 
• Cooperate during verification. 
• Keep complaint reference numbers safe. 
• Monitor grievance status regularly.
